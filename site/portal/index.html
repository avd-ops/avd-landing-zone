        Topology manifest JSON & how sizing works

        It starts from the minimum viable kit: one memory-optimized E4as_v5 host and a 100 GiB profile share. Automatic sizes pooled hosts from how many people use them and how they work (Microsoft's multi-session guidance: light 6, medium 4, heavy 2, power 1 user per vCPU). Personal pools assign one dedicated host per user.

        Once you apply a sizing and topology, the commands carry it: the pre-deployment preflight checks quota, capacity and existing Private DNS zones, and deploy.sh --topology deploys every workspace, application group, host pool, Private Endpoint and Private DNS zone.

        Cost ,

        Hosts bill for the hours they run. These two settings decide those hours.

        How the cost is worked out

        Most of the cost is the hours the session hosts run. Both settings are deployed with the landing zone. The pre-deployment preflight prices the plan at Azure list prices for your region; after that, changing a setting here reprices it without running anything.
